Branch on what readers do

Condition splits on events, opens, profile fields, segments and subscriptions. A/B splits with fixed weights. Wait-for-event with a timeout path.

Start from anything

A segment when you activate it, an event from the SDK or your backend, or a new article arriving in an RSS or Atom feed.

Send at each reader's best hour

Send-time optimisation learns when each reader usually opens and delivers within the next 24 hours.

Newsletters and topics built in

Opt-in newsletters, opt-out topics, a preference centre, quiet hours and frequency caps apply to every send.

Why own it.

For most companies, messaging is a marketing tool. For a newsroom it is how the story reaches people first, and where the reader relationship lives.

Data

Reader data stays with you

Device tokens, reading history and subscriptions stay in your cloud account. There is no new data processor to assess and no cross-border transfer.

Speed

Your alert goes first

Your own queue and workers, not a shared one. Breaking alerts jump the line, and you add capacity before an election night.

Cost

Costs follow infrastructure

Vendor bills grow with monthly active readers and message volume. Dispatch costs a small team and the cloud it runs on.

Fit

Built for news

Feed-driven sends, Live Activities, per-language templates and article recommendations are core features, not paid add-ons.

Stack

Plugs into what you run

Sign-in through your identity provider, nightly loads into your warehouse, alarms in CloudWatch, and an API your CMS can call.

Exit

Nothing to migrate away from

You keep the SDKs, the data model and the history. No re-integration project in every app when a contract ends.

Running in your cloud this week.

Dispatch ships with Terraform for AWS and a one-command deploy. Your apps add a small SDK, and editors sign in with the identity provider you already use.

STEP 1

Provision

Apply the Terraform: network, Postgres, containers, email and alarms, in your AWS account.

STEP 2

Deploy

Build and roll out with one script. Migrations run before new code takes traffic.

STEP 3

Connect sign-in

Point SAML or OIDC at your identity provider and map its groups to admin rights.

STEP 4

Add the SDK

Drop the SDK into your apps and site, register for push, and send your first alert.
